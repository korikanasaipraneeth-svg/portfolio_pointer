## 🚀 Verified Flagship Deployments

### 1. [STUDENT360 — Academic SaaS & Verification Engine](https://github.com/korikanasaipraneeth-svg/student-portfolio)
- **Problem Solved:** Resume inflation and unverified academic claims for recruiters seeking engineering talent.

- **Features:** Faculty cryptographic credential endorsement, interactive Kanban candidate hiring board, and automated portfolio compilation.
- **Stack:** TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, Express, Tailwind CSS, PostgreSQL.

### 2. [AgriSahay AI — Smart Agricultural Diagnostics](https://github.com/korikanasaipraneeth-svg/project-kissan)
- **Problem Solved:** Delayed crop pathology diagnosis and linguistic barriers for rural smallholder farmers.
- **Architecture:** Client-side camera image capture with Flask/Python computer vision lesion segmentation and Google Gemini Multimodal APIs.
- **Features:** Instant crop pathology detection (<350ms), vernacular conversational advisory in regional dialects, and localized market pricing analytics.
- **Stack:** Python, Flask, OpenCV, Google Gemini API, React, REST Microservices.

### 3. [Guardian AI — Emergency SOS & Geospatial Health Mesh](https://github.com/korikanasaipraneeth-svg/guardian-ai)
- **Problem Solved:** Critical emergency response latency and navigating to nearest trauma centers under distress.
- **Architecture:** Flutter mobile cross-platform client with Python asynchronous backend and Haversine spherical distance calculation.
- **Features:** 1-touch instant SOS trigger with GPS capture, live telemetry streaming, and automated top-3 nearest hospital spatial routing.
- **Stack:** Flutter, Dart, Python, MySQL, Geospatial APIs.
